# One-neighborhood pilot and exception policy

This is a proposed, unfunded pilot policy, not evidence of operating customers, hired workers, insurer coverage, partnerships or viable margin. Initial scope: Cambridge around Garden Street, one enrolled verifier, one independent reviewer, a consented visual visit. Candidate acquisition: incoming students through housing groups with moderator permission. No relationship with a university or listing owner is established.

## Before accepting a pilot booking

Secure renter and property-contact consent, arrange an accessible time and safe meeting plan, and confirm each question or visible exclusion. The reviewer must not be the verifier or listing contact. Do not accept ownership, structural safety or legal certification work. Set a funded pilot reserve **before** promising compensation; the application currently records obligations but does not hold a reserve or send payouts. Do not dispatch real work from this demo.

## Exceptions and compensation

| Event | Renter outcome | Verifier outcome | Operator action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Access denied or unsafe | Incomplete service, authorization void; unresolved void remains visibly unknown | No automatic $40 completed-service eligibility. Any travel stipend must be separately agreed and prepaid from pilot reserve, not charged to renter's service fee | Document contact attempt, reason, travel time; no unsafe retry |
| Incomplete photo coverage | Return with task IDs and exact correction notes while service window remains open; otherwise void | Opportunity to remedy within scope; record elapsed work | Independently inspect new evidence; no broadening accepted scope |
| Good-faith completed work, adverse finding | Full scoped fee after independent review; unfavorable property does not mean failed work | Fixed $40 earning remains pending through appeal window | Publish evidence and preserve uncertainty |
| Completed work, unknown capture | Report available; no duplicate charge | Completed-work obligation remains recorded, payout not submitted | Reconcile existing IDs. If collection finally fails, fund the obligation from reserve; do not claim payout until externally verified |
| Valid refund after good-faith completed work | Refund only from confirmed capture, tracked separately | Hold during appeal; proposed policy pays accepted good-faith work from reserve despite renter refund, after an independent determination | Demo does not automate reserve-funded compensation. Record reserve approval and actual payment separately before releasing any obligation |
| Fabricated or materially non-delivered work | Independently determine refund/void based on actual provider state | Hold disputed earning pending investigation; never label paid | Preserve evidence; give verifier explanation and review channel |
| Provider outcome unresolved | Explain unknown status; no success claim | No funded-payment claim | Inspect dashboard, retain IDs, reconcile; unknown order creation is manual recovery |

Pilot fees are illustrative: $60 renter fee, $40 earning, $20 **before** travel subsidy, provider fees, reviewer time, recruitment, support, refunds, reserves and taxes. Do not call $20 profit or validated margin.

## Consented evidence and comprehension study

Use one real consented visit or clearly labeled realistic staged room/appliance photographs. Preserve listing claim, scoped question, an unfavorable observation, and an uncertainty. Do not capture people, personal documents, passwords or private belongings without consent. Show the photo report to five consenting target renters and one verifier; record each answer verbatim with permission:

1. What did this report add beyond the listing? Point to the photo supporting your conclusion.
2. Which question remains unanswered? What must you ask the listing contact next?
3. Does completed service mean the apartment is safe or the landlord authorized? Explain.
4. Would you pay the stated $60 for this package? Why or why not? Hypothetical willingness is not a sale.
5. Can the verifier execute each instruction and understand a correction without another conversation?

Record confusing or failed answers. Proposed continue criterion: most participants identify the discrepancy and one uncertainty without treating the report as a property endorsement; workable consent/access and measured costs must also be demonstrated. No responses have been collected.

## Submission rehearsal

Main 2:30 narrative: 0:00–0:20 remote renter and concrete concern; 0:20–0:45 accepted question scope/hold; 0:45–1:25 actual labeled evidence and adverse finding; 1:25–1:55 independent completion/capture; 1:55–2:20 renter photo report, limits and separate earning; 2:20–2:30 what remains unverified. Show simulation labels. Keep timeout/reconcile, appeals, clock advance, and safe-stop demonstrations in supplemental footage. Ask an unfamiliar viewer to explain the service and payment trigger, record confusion, and revise before publishing. A written plan is not a completed recording or rehearsal.
